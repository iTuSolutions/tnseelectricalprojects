Solar Repair FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of solar problems can be investigated?

Problems may involve panels, inverters, batteries, electrical connections, system warnings, generation or other system components. The actual repair scope depends on the findings.

Can you troubleshoot an existing solar system?

Existing systems can be assessed subject to their condition, configuration, accessibility and the nature of the reported fault.

Do you repair solar inverters?

TNSE can investigate inverter-related faults and determine the appropriate troubleshooting, repair or replacement requirements.

What information should I provide when requesting a repair?

If available, provide the type of solar system, inverter and battery information, the problem you have noticed and any warning or error messages displayed by the system.

How do I request a solar repair quotation?

Contact TNSE Electrical Projects and provide details about the solar system and the problem requiring attention.
