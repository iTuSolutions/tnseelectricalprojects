About TNSE

Building dependable power solutions for today and tomorrow.

TNSE Electrical Projects was established in 2017 with a focus on providing practical electrical solutions and supporting clients with their power requirements.

Our services cover electrical installations, maintenance, repairs, fault finding, backup power, and related energy solutions.

We understand that every project has different requirements. Our approach is therefore centred on understanding the client's needs, assessing the project requirements and providing a practical solution that supports reliability, safety and long-term value.
