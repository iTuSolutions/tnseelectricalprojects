PROJECT FAQ

Questions about starting a project?

What types of projects does TNSE handle? expand_more

TNSE Electrical Projects focuses on electrical services, solar energy solutions and broader energy-related requirements such as backup power and battery storage.

Can I request a quotation for a new project? expand_more

Yes. You can use the quotation form on the contact page to provide your contact details, service requirement and project information.

Can I send photographs of my existing system? expand_more

When discussing a project, photographs and other relevant information may help provide useful context about the existing installation or site requirement.

Does TNSE provide maintenance after installation? expand_more

TNSE offers electrical and solar maintenance services. The appropriate support depends on the system and project requirements.
