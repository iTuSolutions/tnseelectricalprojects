SAFETY FOCUS

Electrical installations require appropriate safety procedures.

Electrical installation work involves electrical equipment and systems that can present safety risks. Installation, testing and repair work should be carried out using appropriate safety procedures and by suitably qualified personnel.

INSTALLATION FAQ

Questions about electrical installations?

What electrical installation services does TNSE provide? expand_more

TNSE provides electrical installation services that can include wiring, lighting, electrical connections, distribution boards and related electrical infrastructure according to the project scope.

Do you provide residential electrical installations? expand_more

Yes. Residential installation requirements can include wiring, lighting, outlets, distribution and other electrical requirements, subject to assessment.

Do you handle commercial installations? expand_more

TNSE provides electrical installation services for suitable commercial environments based on the requirements and scope of each project.

Can you work on an existing electrical installation? expand_more

Existing electrical installations can be assessed to determine whether installation, maintenance, repair or other electrical work may be required.

How do I request an installation quotation? expand_more

Contact TNSE with your location, project requirements, type of property and any available information about the proposed electrical installation.
