About TNSE Electrical Projects

Practical power solutions built around your needs.

TNSE Electrical Projects is an electrical and solar energy solutions company established in 2017 and based in Moletjie, Polokwane, Limpopo.

We provide electrical installation, maintenance, fault finding, repairs, solar installation and energy-related solutions for a range of residential, commercial and project requirements.
