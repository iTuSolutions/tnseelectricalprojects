Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding our professional electrical engineering, solar integration, and backup power solutions across Polokwane and Limpopo.

What electrical services does TNSE provide? expand_more

TNSE delivers comprehensive electrical services, including complete wiring, lighting design, distribution board upgrades, maintenance, and expert fault finding for residential and commercial sites.

What does a professional solar installation include? expand_more

Our solar solutions incorporate high-efficiency photovoltaic panels, hybrid inverters, secure battery storage, professional electrical integration, system configuration, rigorous testing, and handover.

Can solar generation be combined with battery storage? expand_more

Yes. Solar panels and lithium battery storage seamlessly integrate to provide reliable backup power during utility outages and optimize energy independence for your property.

Do you handle residential and commercial properties? expand_more

Yes. We engineer customized electrical and solar systems tailored specifically to the unique energy demands and scale of residential homes, commercial businesses, and facilities.

Do you provide troubleshooting and repair support? expand_more

Yes. We offer expert diagnostic and repair services for existing electrical faults, solar system warnings, inverter errors, and battery storage performance issues.

Can TNSE assess or work on existing installations? expand_more

Existing electrical and solar infrastructure can be professionally assessed regarding its current condition, capacity, and configuration to determine necessary upgrades or maintenance.

What backup power solutions are available? expand_more

We design power continuity systems that support essential loads during interruptions, protecting critical appliances and business operations through advanced storage solutions.

Do you offer post-installation maintenance? expand_more

We offer ongoing electrical and solar system maintenance plans to ensure peak performance, longevity, and safety across all completed installations.

What details should I provide when requesting a quote? expand_more

Please share your location, property type, specific service requirements, and any relevant details or photographs regarding your current electrical or solar setup.

How do I request a project consultation or quote? expand_more

You can easily reach out through our contact page or request a quote directly to discuss your project scope, site assessment, and tailored engineering requirements.
