FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Energy solutions questions

What type of energy solutions does TNSE provide? expand_more

TNSE provides energy-related solutions including backup power, battery storage, renewable energy integration, solar solutions, power continuity and energy-efficiency approaches.

Can TNSE help with backup power? expand_more

Yes. TNSE can assess your backup requirements and help determine a suitable approach for supporting essential loads during power interruptions.

Can solar and battery storage work together? expand_more

Solar generation and battery storage can be integrated into suitable systems. The appropriate configuration depends on the property's energy requirements and system design.

Do you provide solutions for businesses? expand_more

Yes. TNSE provides electrical, solar, backup and energy solutions that can be considered for commercial and business environments.

How do I request an energy solution? expand_more

Contact TNSE with details about your property, project or energy requirements. We can then discuss the appropriate next steps and quotation requirements.
