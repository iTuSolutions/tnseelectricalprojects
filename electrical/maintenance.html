FAQ

Electrical maintenance questions.

What does electrical maintenance include? expand_more

Depending on the project, electrical maintenance may include inspections, condition checks, fault detection, maintenance of relevant components and other agreed electrical maintenance activities.

Why is preventative maintenance important? expand_more

Planned maintenance provides a structured opportunity to inspect electrical systems and identify conditions that may require attention before they develop into more significant maintenance concerns.

Can you investigate electrical faults? expand_more

Yes. Fault investigation can form part of the maintenance or repair scope. The appropriate approach depends on the reported problem and the condition of the electrical system.

Do you maintain existing electrical installations? expand_more

Existing installations can be assessed to determine the appropriate maintenance requirements, subject to site conditions and project scope.

How can I request electrical maintenance? expand_more

Contact TNSE Electrical Projects with details about your property, electrical system or maintenance requirement. The information can then be reviewed to determine the appropriate next step.
