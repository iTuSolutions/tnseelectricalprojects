SOLAR SOLUTIONS

A practical approach to solar energy

Solar energy can form an important part of a property's overall energy strategy. TNSE Electrical Projects provides solar-related solutions covering system planning, installation, maintenance, repairs, battery storage and backup power.

We focus on understanding the energy requirements of each project before determining a suitable approach. System requirements can vary depending on electricity usage, available space, existing infrastructure and backup requirements.

Our solar services can be considered for homes, businesses, properties and suitable projects requiring renewable energy and power-continuity solutions.
