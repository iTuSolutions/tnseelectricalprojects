ELECTRICAL REPAIRS

Find the problem before deciding on the repair.

Electrical problems can originate from different parts of an installation. A proper repair process therefore begins with understanding the reported symptoms and assessing the affected electrical system.

TNSE Electrical Projects can provide fault finding, troubleshooting, diagnosis and corrective repair support according to the condition of the system and the agreed project scope.

engineering

A structured repair approach

Rather than treating every electrical problem the same way, the repair process considers the symptoms, system condition, affected components and appropriate corrective action.

Electrical safety notice

Electrical faults can present serious safety risks. Do not attempt to open, modify or repair electrical equipment if you are not suitably qualified to do so. Where an electrical problem is suspected, appropriate safety procedures should be followed and suitably qualified personnel should be engaged.

FAQ

Electrical repair questions.

What electrical repair services do you provide? expand_more

Services can include electrical fault finding, troubleshooting, fault diagnosis, component repair or replacement support and other corrective electrical work, depending on the system and agreed scope.

Can you find the cause of an electrical fault? expand_more

Fault finding and diagnosis can be used to investigate the source of reported electrical problems. The extent of diagnosis depends on the nature and condition of the electrical system.

Do you repair electrical components? expand_more

Where appropriate, affected electrical components can be assessed for repair or replacement. The recommended option depends on the condition of the component and the wider electrical system.

Do you handle urgent electrical repair requests? expand_more

Urgent repair requests can be considered subject to availability, safety requirements and the nature of the electrical problem.

How do I request an electrical repair? expand_more

Contact TNSE Electrical Projects with information about the electrical problem, affected equipment or property and any relevant symptoms. This helps establish the appropriate next step.
