SOLAR PROJECT FAQ

Questions about solar projects?

What types of solar projects does TNSE provide? expand_more

TNSE provides solar-related services including solar installations, system integration, inverter and battery projects, backup power solutions, maintenance and repairs.

Can TNSE install solar for homes? expand_more

Residential solar installations can be considered based on the property's requirements, existing electrical installation and suitable system configuration.

Do solar projects include battery storage? expand_more

Battery storage can form part of a solar or backup power system where it is appropriate for the project requirements.

Can TNSE work on an existing solar system? expand_more

Existing solar systems can be assessed for applicable maintenance, troubleshooting or repair requirements.

How do I request a solar quotation? expand_more

Contact TNSE and provide your project requirements, location, contact details and any useful information about your existing or proposed solar system.
